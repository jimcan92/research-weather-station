## ⚠️ IMPORTANT NOTES

1. **R3 = 390kΩ** — check jud! Dili 330k (para Li-Ion na). 390k para LiFePO4 14.6V full charge
2. **LM2596-5.0 FIXED version** — ayaw palit ug adjustable (naay pot). Ang fixed version walay tuning needed
3. **4S BMS with BALANCE** — dapat naay balance wires (5 wires: B-, B1, B2, B3, B4+). Kung 3-wire ra, walay balance
4. **32700 cells** — ayaw i-solder directly ang terminals. Use holder or spot weld with nickel strips
5. **SS34 SMD** — kung di ka comfortable sa SMD, pwede 1N5822 through-hole (same 3A/40V Schottky)
6. **Fuse 10A** between solar panel and charge controller. **Fuse 5A** (PTC sa PCB) between battery and power board
7. **Gamay ra nga BMS** — 5-10A BMS igo na for this setup (300mA load ra). Ang 30A BMS parehas ra ug price so palita nalang daan
